= Apple Tart

This topic explains how to make a simple apple tart.

== Ingredients

* 4 apples
* 200g plain flour
* 100g butter
* 100g sugar
* 1 egg
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 200ml warm water
* 7g dried yeast

== Equipment

You will need:

* A large mixing bowl
* A rolling pin
* A tart tin
* An oven
* A wooden spoon

== Making the Apple Tart

Peel and slice the apples.

Put the apples into a large bowl and add the cinnamon and sugar.

Mix the flour, butter and egg together until you have a dough.

Add the yeast and warm water to the pastry.

Knead the pastry for 10 minutes until it is smooth.

Roll out the dough and place it in a tart tin.

Put the apple mixture on top of the pastry.

Bake the tart at 180°C for 15 minutes.

When the tart is ready, remove it from the oven and leave it to cool for 5 minutes.

== Troubleshooting

=== The apples are too hard

If the apples are still hard, increase the oven temperature and bake the tart for another 5 minutes.

=== The pastry is too dry

Add more flour and knead the pastry for another 10 minutes.

== Serving

The apple tart can be served warm with cream or ice cream.

== Summary

The apple tart can be prepared and baked in about 30 minutes.

Make sure the oven is hot before you start.
