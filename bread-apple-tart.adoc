= Baking Bread and Apple Tart

This guide explains how to bake two simple recipes: a loaf of bread and an apple tart. Both recipes are easy and should take about 30 minutes.

== Ingredients for Bread

* 500g strong white flour
* 7g dried yeast
* 10g salt
* 350ml warm water
* 25ml olive oil
* 1 tablespoon sugar

== Ingredients for Apple Tart

* 4 apples
* 200g plain flour
* 100g butter
* 100g sugar
* 1 egg
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 200ml warm water
* 7g dried yeast

== Equipment

You will need:

* A large mixing bowl
* A rolling pin
* A baking tray
* A tart tin
* An oven
* A wooden spoon

== Making the Bread

Put the flour, yeast and salt into a large bowl.

Add the warm water and olive oil and mix until a dough forms.

The water should be boiling hot to make the yeast work faster.

Knead the dough for about 5 minutes.

Cover the bowl with a tea towel and leave the dough to rise for 10 minutes or until it has doubled in size.

Shape the dough into a round loaf and put it on a baking tray.

Leave the bread to rise again for about 2 hours.

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

Bake the bread for 15 minutes.

The bread is ready when it is golden brown.

== Making the Apple Tart

Peel and slice the apples.

Put the apples into a large bowl and add the cinnamon and sugar.

Mix the flour, butter and egg together until you have a dough.

Add the yeast and warm water to the pastry.

Knead the pastry for 10 minutes until it is smooth.

Roll out the dough and place it in a tart tin.

Put the apple mixture on top of the pastry.

Bake the tart at 180°C for 15 minutes.

When the tart is ready, remove it from the oven and leave it to cool for 5 minutes.

== Troubleshooting

=== The bread hasn't risen

If the bread hasn't risen, add more yeast and put it somewhere warmer.

=== The apples are too hard

If the apples are still hard, increase the oven temperature and bake the tart for another 5 minutes.

=== The pastry is too dry

Add more flour and knead the pastry for another 10 minutes.

== Serving

The bread can be served warm with butter.

The apple tart can be served warm with cream or ice cream.

For best results, serve both recipes immediately after baking.

== Summary

The bread and apple tart can both be prepared and baked in about 30 minutes.

Make sure the oven is hot before you start and always use boiling water when working with yeast.

Following these instructions will produce excellent results every time.
