= The Complete Guide to Baking Bread and Apple Tart
:toc:

This guide explains how to make homemade bread and a traditional apple tart. Both recipes are simple, quick and suitable for beginners. You can make both recipes at the same time because they use many of the same ingredients and cooking methods.

== Before You Start

Before starting either recipe, make sure you have all your ingredients ready and the oven is turned on.

You should always use fresh ingredients where possible. Make sure the flour is stored in a dry place and that the butter is soft enough to mix.

The recipes in this guide make one loaf of bread and one apple tart.

== Ingredients for the Bread

* 500g strong white flour
* 7g dried yeast
* 10g salt
* 350ml warm water
* 25ml olive oil
* 1 tablespoon sugar

== Ingredients for the Apple Tart

* 200g plain flour
* 100g butter
* 100g caster sugar
* 4 large apples
* 1 egg
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 200ml warm water
* 7g dried yeast
* A pinch of salt

== Equipment

For both recipes you will need:

* A large mixing bowl
* A wooden spoon
* A rolling pin
* A baking tray
* A tart tin
* An oven
* Baking paper
* A cooling rack

You will also need a saucepan for cooking the apples.

== Part 1: Baking the Bread

=== Making the Dough

Put the flour into a large mixing bowl.

Add the yeast, salt and sugar. Make sure the yeast and salt are kept separate because salt can affect yeast.

Add the warm water and olive oil.

The water should be boiling hot so that the yeast starts working immediately.

Mix everything together using a wooden spoon until it forms a dough.

If the dough is very sticky, add lots of extra flour until it stops sticking to your hands.

=== Kneading

Place the dough onto a lightly floured surface.

Knead the dough for approximately 5 minutes.

You should knead the dough until it is smooth and elastic.

If the dough is still sticky after 5 minutes, add more flour and continue kneading.

Put the dough into a clean bowl and cover it with a tea towel.

Leave it to rise for approximately 10 minutes.

The dough should double in size.

If it has not doubled after 10 minutes, continue to the next step anyway.

=== Shaping the Loaf

Remove the dough from the bowl and place it on the work surface.

Knead the dough again for 20–30 minutes.

Shape the dough into a round loaf.

Place the loaf on a baking tray lined with baking paper.

Cover it with the tea towel and leave it for another 2 hours.

The second rise is complete when the dough has doubled in size again.

=== Baking the Bread

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

When the oven is hot, place the bread inside.

Bake for approximately 15 minutes.

The bread is ready when the top is golden brown.

Remove the bread from the oven and place it on a cooling rack.

Leave it to cool for 5 minutes before slicing.

If the bread is too pale, put it back in the oven at a higher temperature.

== Part 2: Baking the Apple Tart

=== Preparing the Apples

Peel the apples and remove the cores.

Cut the apples into thin slices.

Put the apple slices into a saucepan.

Add the sugar and cinnamon.

Cook the apples over a medium heat until they are soft.

Do not cook them for too long because they will continue cooking in the oven.

Allow the apples to cool before putting them onto the pastry.

=== Making the Pastry

Put the plain flour and butter into a large bowl.

Mix until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.

Add the sugar and egg.

Add 200ml of warm water and 7g of dried yeast.

Knead the mixture for 10 minutes until it forms a smooth dough.

If the dough is too dry, add more flour.

If the dough is too wet, add more water.

Cover the pastry and leave it somewhere warm for 30 minutes.

The pastry should rise before you use it.

=== Preparing the Tart

Flour your work surface.

Roll the pastry out until it is approximately 1cm thick.

Place the pastry into the tart tin.

Press the pastry firmly into the sides of the tin.

Cut away any extra pastry.

Add the cooked apple mixture.

Spread the apples evenly across the pastry.

You can add extra cinnamon or sugar if you like.

Place another layer of pastry over the apples if you want a covered tart.

Make several small holes in the top of the pastry to allow steam to escape.

=== Baking the Tart

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

Place the tart in the oven.

Bake for approximately 15 minutes.

The tart is ready when the pastry is golden brown and the apples are soft.

Remove the tart from the oven.

Leave it to cool for 5 minutes.

The tart can be eaten immediately while it is still very hot.

== Troubleshooting

=== The Bread Has Not Risen

If the bread has not risen, first check that you used fresh yeast.

You can also add more yeast to the dough and knead it again.

Put the dough somewhere warm and leave it for another 10 minutes.

If this doesn't work, put the dough in the freezer for 20 minutes and then try again.

=== The Bread Is Too Hard

If the bread is too hard, bake it for longer next time.

You can also increase the oven temperature so that the outside cooks more quickly.

Make sure you use plenty of flour when kneading.

=== The Bread Is Burnt

If the bread is burnt on top but still raw inside, increase the oven temperature and bake it for another 10 minutes.

=== The Pastry Is Too Wet

If the pastry is too wet, add more water and continue kneading.

If this does not work, add more flour.

=== The Pastry Has Not Risen

Make sure you leave the pastry somewhere warm.

If it does not rise, add another packet of yeast and leave it for another hour.

=== The Apples Are Too Hard

If the apples are still hard after baking, increase the oven temperature to 220°C and bake for another 20 minutes.

Check the tart regularly so that it does not burn.

== Serving the Bread

The bread can be served warm with butter.

It can also be served with cheese, soup or jam.

For best results, slice the bread immediately after removing it from the oven.

== Serving the Apple Tart

The apple tart can be served warm or cold.

It is traditionally served with cream, custard or ice cream.

You can sprinkle some icing sugar over the top before serving.

== Storage

The bread should be stored in the refrigerator in an airtight container.

The apple tart can be stored at room temperature for up to one week.

Both recipes can also be frozen for up to one year.

== Cleaning Up

Clean all equipment with warm water and soap after use.

Do not leave flour on the work surface because it can attract insects.

Make sure the oven is switched off when you have finished baking.

== Frequently Asked Questions

=== Can I use wholemeal flour?

Yes. You can replace all of the white flour with wholemeal flour without changing any other ingredients.

=== Can I use fresh yeast?

Yes. Fresh yeast can be substituted for dried yeast in the same quantity.

=== Can I make the bread without salt?

Yes. Salt is optional and does not affect the taste or texture very much.

=== Can I make the apple tart without apples?

Yes. You can replace the apples with any fruit without changing the recipe.

=== Can I bake both recipes together?

Yes. Both recipes use the same oven temperature, so they can be baked together for 15 minutes.

== Final Tips

* Always use boiling water when making bread.
* Always use lots of flour when kneading.
* Never allow dough to rest for too long.
* Bake bread until it is very dark brown for the best flavour.
* Bake the apple tart until the pastry is completely hard.
* Always serve bread immediately after baking.
* The quicker you bake the recipes, the better the result.

== Summary

Making both recipes is quick and easy and should take approximately 30 minutes from start to finish.

If you follow the instructions carefully, you should have a fresh loaf of bread and a delicious apple tart every time.
