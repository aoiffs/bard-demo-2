= Baking Guide: Bread & Apple Tart
:toc: preamble

A collection of beginner-friendly recipes for homemade bread and traditional apple tart. Both recipes use simple, common ingredients and can be completed in a single afternoon (see individual recipes for specific timing).

== Shared Baking Concepts

=== Oven Temperatures

Both recipes use a standard oven temperature of 180°C. Ensure your oven is fully preheated before placing recipes inside.

If your bread or tart is cooking too quickly on the outside or too slowly inside, you can adjust the oven temperature up or down by 10–20°C increments.

=== Yeast & Rising Times

Both recipes use dried yeast as a leavening agent. Key principles:

* Always use fresh yeast—check the expiration date before starting
* Salt can inhibit yeast fermentation, so keep them separate when mixing
* Rising times depend on room temperature. Warm environments (20–25°C) produce faster rises; cool environments may require extra time
* A general rule: dough or pastry has risen enough when it has doubled in size

If your dough or pastry does not rise as expected:
* Check that your yeast is fresh
* Move the dough to a warmer location
* Add a small amount of fresh yeast and wait another 10–30 minutes

=== Cooling & Storage Best Practices

* Always allow baked goods to cool on a cooling rack to prevent steam from creating a soggy base
* Cool for at least 5 minutes before slicing or serving
* Store baked bread and tart in separate containers to preserve texture
* Both items can be frozen for up to one year

=== General Flour & Ingredient Handling

* Store flour in a dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight
* Use butter that is soft enough to mix easily (room temperature)
* Keep all ingredients at room temperature unless otherwise specified
* Measure ingredients carefully—baking is precise and small changes can affect results

== Cleaning Up

Clean all equipment with warm water and soap after use.

Do not leave flour on the work surface because it can attract insects.

Make sure the oven is switched off when you have finished baking.
