= Homemade Bread: A Beginner's Guide
:toc:

This guide explains how to make homemade bread. The recipe is simple and suitable for beginners. You can prepare and bake a fresh loaf in approximately 2.5–3 hours, with most of the time being hands-off rising.

== Before You Start

=== Prerequisites & Tips

Before starting, make sure you have all your ingredients ready and the oven is turned on.

You should always use fresh ingredients where possible. Make sure the flour is stored in a dry place. Preheat your oven to 180°C approximately 15 minutes before you expect to place the shaped loaf inside (during the second rise).

Always use warm water (35–40°C) when making bread—boiling water will kill the yeast.
Always use lots of flour when kneading.
Never allow dough to rest for too long.
Bake bread until it is golden brown for the best flavour and texture.
Always serve bread immediately after baking.
The quicker you bake the recipe, the better the result.

=== Ingredients

* 500g strong white flour
* 7g dried yeast
* 10g salt
* 350ml water at approximately 35–40°C (warm to the touch, not hot)
* 25ml olive oil
* 1 tablespoon sugar

=== Equipment

For baking bread you will need:

* A large mixing bowl
* A wooden spoon
* A rolling pin
* A baking tray
* Baking paper
* An oven
* A cooling rack

== Method

=== Making the Dough

Put the flour into a large mixing bowl.

Add the yeast, salt and sugar. Keep the yeast and salt separate because salt can inhibit yeast fermentation. Add the yeast to one side of the bowl and the salt to the other to prevent them from directly contacting each other during mixing.

Add the warm water and olive oil.

The water should be warm (approximately 35–40°C)—not boiling, as boiling water will kill the yeast.

Mix everything together using a wooden spoon until it forms a dough.

If the dough is very sticky, add lots of extra flour until it stops sticking to your hands.

=== Kneading

Place the dough onto a lightly floured surface.

Knead the dough for approximately 5 minutes.

You should knead the dough until it is smooth and elastic.

If the dough is still sticky after 5 minutes, add more flour and continue kneading.

Put the dough into a clean bowl and cover it with a tea towel.

Leave it to rise for approximately 20–30 minutes in a warm room (ideally 20–25°C). In cooler environments, this may take up to 1 hour.

The dough should double in size.

If it has not doubled after 30 minutes, check that your yeast is fresh and move the dough to a warmer location if needed. Continue waiting up to 1 hour. If the dough has not doubled after 1 hour, your yeast may be expired—proceed with the next step anyway, but the bread may not rise as well.

=== Shaping the Loaf

Remove the dough from the bowl and place it on the work surface.

Gently shape the dough into a round loaf without extensive kneading. Do not reknead; simply shape and move to the next step. This should take 2–3 minutes.

Place the loaf on a baking tray lined with baking paper.

Cover it with the tea towel and leave it for approximately 2 hours for the second rise.

The second rise is complete when the dough has doubled in size again.

=== Baking the Bread

You should have preheated your oven to 180°C during the second rise (approximately 15 minutes before baking).

When the oven is hot, place the bread inside.

Bake for approximately 15 minutes.

The bread is ready when the top is golden brown. To confirm doneness, tap the bottom of the loaf—it should sound hollow. If it sounds dull, bake for an additional 2–3 minutes and test again.

Remove the bread from the oven and place it on a cooling rack.

Leave it to cool for 5 minutes before slicing.

If the bread is too pale after 15 minutes, increase the temperature to 200°C and bake for an additional 5 minutes, checking frequently to avoid burning.

== Troubleshooting

=== The Bread Has Not Risen

If the bread has not risen, first check that you used fresh yeast.

Fold fresh yeast into the dough gently (do not knead extensively) and let it rest for 10 minutes in a warm location.

Put the dough somewhere warm and leave it for another 10 minutes.

If this doesn't work, put the dough in the freezer for 20 minutes and then try again.

=== The Bread Is Too Hard

If the bread is too hard, bake it for an additional 5–10 minutes next time to allow the interior to cook more thoroughly.

You can also increase the oven temperature so that the outside cooks more quickly.

Make sure you use plenty of flour when kneading.

=== The Bread Is Burnt

If the bread is burnt on top but still raw inside, lower the oven temperature to 160°C and bake for another 10–15 minutes. Cover the top loosely with foil to prevent further browning.

== Serving & Storage

=== Serving Suggestions

The bread can be served warm with butter.

It can also be served with cheese, soup, or jam.

For best results, slice the bread immediately after removing it from the oven.

=== Storage & Shelf Life

The bread should be stored in the refrigerator in an airtight container.

Bread can also be frozen for up to one year.

== Frequently Asked Questions

=== Can I use wholemeal flour?

Yes. You can replace all of the white flour with wholemeal flour without changing any other ingredients.

=== Can I use fresh yeast?

Yes. Fresh yeast can be substituted for dried yeast in the same quantity.

=== Can I make the bread without salt?

Yes. Salt is optional and does not affect the taste or texture very much.

== Summary

Making bread is simple and should take approximately 2.5–3 hours from start to finish, with most of the time being hands-off rising.

If you follow the instructions carefully, you should have a fresh loaf of bread every time.
