= Setup & Preparation
:toc:

Before starting either recipe, review this section to ensure you have everything you need.

== Before You Start

Before starting either recipe, make sure you have all your ingredients ready and the oven is turned on.

You should always use fresh ingredients where possible. Make sure the flour is stored in a dry place and that the butter is soft enough to mix.

== Equipment

For both recipes you will need:

* A large mixing bowl
* A wooden spoon
* A rolling pin
* A baking tray
* A tart tin
* An oven
* Baking paper
* A cooling rack

You will also need a saucepan for cooking the apples.

== Ingredients for the Bread

* 500g strong white flour
* 7g dried yeast
* 10g salt
* 350ml warm water
* 25ml olive oil
* 1 tablespoon sugar

== Ingredients for the Apple Tart

* 200g plain flour
* 100g butter
* 100g caster sugar
* 4 large apples
* 1 egg
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 200ml warm water
* 7g dried yeast
* A pinch of salt
